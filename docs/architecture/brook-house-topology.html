Architecture — 2026-11-02

Brook House Topology

Three machines on one tailnet run Andre's personal assistant. futileassistant holds the data and the reasoning, vocalassistant is the ears and mouth in the living room, homeassistant routes the house. Everything inside the dashed boundary only talks to itself over Tailscale; the one way in from outside is the Funnel-exposed MCP endpoint.

Data & reasoning — MCP tools, Postgres, Claude Sonnet 5 calls
Human I/O — voice pipeline, Signal messaging
Durability — nightly encrypted backups
